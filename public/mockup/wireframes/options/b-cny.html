Questions, answered

How is this different from the hotel's in-house AV?

For a short speech in a small room, the venue's own setup may be enough. For a full programme with a lo hei, performances and a lucky draw, we bring the LED wall, stage and sound together and run them with our team.

How much does AV for a CNY dinner cost?

We quote per event. Equipment is charged per rental day, and technician charges depend on the hours your programme runs.

Can you show our lucky draw on the screen?

Yes. Send us your lucky-draw graphics and videos and we run them on the LED wall on cue, with a walk-up mic for the winners.

What if something goes wrong during the dinner?

Our technicians stay on standby throughout, with spare LED panels and backup mics on site.
