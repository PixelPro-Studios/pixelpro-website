Questions, answered

What affects your quotation

  • Number of rental days
  • Screen size, indoor or outdoor
  • Venue access and setup timing
  • Technician hours on the day
How much does LED wall rental cost?

Every event is different, so we quote per event. Most equipment is charged per rental day, and technician charges depend on the working hours you need.

How big can the LED wall be?

Whatever size you have in mind, we can build it. Common sizes include 6 m × 4 m and 8 m × 3 m. Book a free consultation and we'll recommend a size for your venue.

Which LED wall suits outdoor events?

We use P2.5 panels indoors and P3.9 panels for outdoor events and bright venues.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. LEW and PE endorsements where required, and all necessary permits handled by us, tailored to your venue.

Do you work with schools?

We regularly work with MOE schools and understand their procurement processes. As a GeBIZ-registered vendor listed on GeBIZ Mall, we support purchases through GeBIZ and SAP Ariba, as well as e-invoicing.

LED wall or projector?

LED walls stay bright on big stages, outdoors and in well-lit rooms. Projectors suit smaller rooms with controlled lighting. We provide both, so we'll recommend what fits your event.
