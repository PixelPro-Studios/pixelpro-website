Questions, answered

How much does LED wall rental cost?

Every event is different, so we quote per event. Most equipment is charged per rental day, and technician charges depend on the working hours you need.

How big can the LED wall be?

Whatever size you have in mind, we can build it. Common sizes include 6 m × 4 m and 8 m × 3 m.

Which LED wall suits outdoor events?

We use P2.5 panels indoors and P3.9 panels for outdoor events and bright venues.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. LEW and PE endorsements where required, and all necessary permits handled by us, tailored to your venue.

How far ahead should we book?

At least two to four weeks for standard events, and earlier in peak season. For urgent enquiries, give us a call.

LED wall or projector?

LED walls stay bright on big stages, outdoors and in well-lit rooms. Projectors suit smaller rooms with controlled lighting. We provide both.
