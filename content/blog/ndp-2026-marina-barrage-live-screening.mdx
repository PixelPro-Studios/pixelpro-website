NDP 2026 is heading back to the **National Stadium** at the Singapore Sports Hub, the first time in a decade the parade returns to Kallang. That means a bigger indoor show, a new fireworks geography over the Kallang Basin, and one practical question for everyone without a ticket:

Where do you still go to celebrate Singapore’s 61st birthday together?

One answer: **Marina Barrage**. Spread out on the green roof, watch the parade live on a large LED wall, and enjoy an open-air National Day evening with music before and after the stream.

## NDP 2026 is at the Sports Hub: what that changes

This year’s parade is not at the Padang. It’s at the National Stadium under the theme **“Majulah Singapura, Go Beyond!”**, with tens of thousands of seats inside the venue.

That shift matters if you’re planning a night out:

- **Ticketed show:** National Stadium, Kallang
- **Fireworks focus:** Kallang Basin (not the usual Marina Bay waterfront)
- **Public precinct buzz:** NDP Celebrations around The Kallang in the lead-up to 9 August
- **No stadium ticket?** Livestream viewing spots like Marina Barrage become more useful, not less

If fireworks are your only goal, head toward Kallang Basin spots such as Stadium Riverside Walk or Tanjong Rhu Promenade. If you want a big-screen parade experience, picnic space, and a festive crowd away from the stadium crush, Marina Barrage is built for that.

## What’s happening at Marina Barrage

As part of PUB’s flagship community celebrations, Marina Barrage hosts a National Day gathering centred on a **live LED wall broadcast** of the parade.

Expect an afternoon arrival on the green roof, a livestream that follows the official NDP broadcast through the evening, and a wind-down once the show wraps. Exact programme timings can shift closer to the day. Treat the broadcast window as “when the official stream runs,” and arrive early if you want space on the grass.

Around the screening, this year’s setup also includes:

- **Sound across Levels 1 and 2**, so the broadcast and music carry through the venue, not only the roof
- **DJ set before and after the stream**, an added layer beyond a silent screening
- **Activities downstairs**, including community booths and programmes on the lower level

## Why the green roof still works in a Sports Hub year

Every National Day, the Barrage’s green roof draws a huge crowd for the same reasons:

- Free entry and picnic-friendly lawn space
- Skyline views and an open-air, under-the-stars atmosphere
- Easy enough access from Marina Bay without needing Sports Hub tickets
- A shared viewing experience for families and friend groups

In a year when the parade is indoors at Kallang, a livestream venue with music and room to sit becomes one of the simplest ways to still feel part of National Day together.

## Can you see the fireworks from Marina Barrage?

Short answer for 2026: **don’t count on it**.

With NDP back at the National Stadium, the main fireworks are launched over the **Kallang Basin**. Marina Barrage is a better pick for the **live parade screening and atmosphere** than for a front-row fireworks view.

If fireworks are the priority, plan for Kallang. If the priority is watching the show on a big LED wall with friends on the grass, Marina Barrage is the move.

## Practical tips before you go

1. **Bring a mat.** Green roof seating is grass.
2. **Arrive early.** Popular patches go quickly, especially for groups.
3. **Check the weather.** It’s open-air; pack light rain protection.
4. **Travel light.** Easier in a dense evening crowd.
5. **Plan your exit.** Give yourself time for lifts, exits, and transport after the evening wraps.
6. **Stay hydrated.** August evenings can still run warm before the breeze picks up.

## Getting there

Marina Barrage is at 8 Marina Gardens Drive. Public transport is usually easiest on National Day:

- Nearest options typically include Bayfront or Gardens by the Bay, then a short transfer or walk
- Expect heavier foot traffic around Marina Bay from late afternoon
- Driving and parking are often slower on NDP evening, so leave extra buffer if you must drive

Confirm latest access notes on PUB’s channels closer to the day.
